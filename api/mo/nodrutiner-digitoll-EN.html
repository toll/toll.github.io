Emergency Procedure for Notification and disclosure of Information (MO) Submission

If you have already successfully submitted the notification and information data, you may cross the border as normal, provided that the border crossing solution is operational. If the border crossing solution is also unavailable, you will receive a red light or no signal. In that case, you must contact the Norwegian Customs Administration, either at the customs office for road and ferry transport, or by email (digitoll@toll.no) for air, rail, and sea transport. Please provide identifying information for the means of transport and the operator and await further instructions from the Norwegian Customs Administration.

If you are unable to submit the notification and disclosure of information data:

For road and ferry transport
Proceed to the customs office, report to the customs clearance desk, and provide one of the following references to document the customs procedure the goods will be subject to after crossing the border:
  • declaration-ID
  • consignment number
  • transit MRN
  • for goods being exported from the EU, the Export MRN must also be presented
If no reference can be provided (for example, for VOEC goods or consolidated customs clearance), the customs office will assess the case individually.
 
For air, rail and sea
Send the same information by email (digitoll@toll.no) later than the time the means of transport crosses the border. Enter Emergency Procedure in the subject line and specify the mode of transport.
 
If you are unable to submit a Tvinn declaration
Follow the TVINN emergency procedure (currently in Norwegian only).
If both the Notification and Information service and TVINN are unavailable at the same time, the TVINN emergency procedure shall be used.
